Three villas by the sea

Youfu Haishu sits inside Longgu Resort Village, known locally as Jinji Resort Center (look for the big red characters 金鸡度假中心 over the gate). We host guests in three villas: Chrysanthemum Pavilion, Red Flower Pavilion and Rose Pavilion.

Each villa has three floors, five guest rooms and nine beds. Book one room, or take the whole villa for a family reunion or a group of friends.

About 30 minutes by car from Fuzhou Changle International Airport and 40 minutes from downtown Fuzhou. Parking is free for guests.

Around the villa

Langqi Island sits where the Min River meets the sea.

  • The beachRight outside the gate. Go tide-pooling at low tide; sand toys can be borrowed or bought.
  • Hill trailStone Arhat statues line the path; the summit looks out over the sea and the bridge.
  • Old archway and pierBuy fresh seafood at the pier and grill it back at the villa.
  • Iron-flower showAbout 20 m from the villa; dates vary with the resort's schedule.
  • Summer poolAt the resort's amusement park, open July–September only.
  • Longan pickingAugust–September, extra charge.
