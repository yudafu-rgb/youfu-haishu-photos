Whole villa or single room

  • Whole villa: three floors, 5 rooms, 9 beds, about 18 guests. The living room, tea room and roof terrace are yours alone, and whole-villa guests may cook in the dining room.
  • Single room: a double room sleeps 2; a twin room (two 1.5 m beds) sleeps 3. Second-floor rooms 201 and 202 look out to the sea at the same rate.
  • Extra guests: ¥50 per person per night above the base number. Children under 12 stay free. No extra beds.
  • Three villas: Juhua (菊花阁), Honghua (红花阁, with projectors) and Meigui (玫瑰阁) stand next to each other and priced the same. You can book two or all three together.

Included in the rate

  • Free parking (scan out at the front desk before you leave)
  • Free jasmine tea in the second-floor tea room
  • Free barbecue grill (bring your own food and charcoal)
  • Pick roselle and lemons in the grounds for free
  • Beach at the front gate; tide-pooling at low tide
  • Karaoke speaker, mahjong table, washing machine
  • Shared gym in the Honghua villa
  • One free date change within the free-cancellation window

Deposit and cancellation

  • Deposit¥100 for a room, ¥500 for a whole villa. Paid at check-in and refunded at check-out after a quick room check.
  • Single roomsFree cancellation until 14:00 the day before arrival; after that, the first night is charged.
  • Whole villaFree cancellation more than 7 days before arrival; 50% refund 3–7 days before; no refund within 3 days.
  • Check-in and check-outCheck-in after 14:00, check-out by 12:00. No smoking indoors. Pets may stay in the garden.
